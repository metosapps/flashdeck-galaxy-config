Privacy Policy

Advertising and privacy choices

FlashDeck uses Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional advertising in Settings. Ads are not displayed on the flashlight controls. Verified FlashDeck Pro users have no ads.

Google may process IP address, advertising or device identifiers, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect advertising, subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements. FlashDeck does not request ads before UMP allows them. Where required, an Ad privacy choices entry in Settings lets you change those choices. Development builds use official Google test ad units only. Production advertising is disabled until separately approved.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies. Google controls retention and deletion for data processed through its services under its policies.

Flashlight, motion and battery

FlashDeck does not request location, contacts or microphone access. Camera permission controls the rear flashlight through Android; the app does not take photos or record videos. Shake gestures and battery readings are processed locally and not transmitted by FlashDeck. There is no separate analytics SDK or FlashDeck user account.

Galaxy purchases

Samsung IAP communicates with Galaxy Store to obtain product information and process purchases. FlashDeck sends the purchase ID over HTTPS to Samsung's receipt service to verify the package, product, payment and test or production mode. FlashDeck does not receive or store payment card details.

Verified purchase and payment IDs are stored locally, separated for test and production and excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Restore requires the original Samsung account. Samsung processes payment and store data under its own policies and store terms.

Remote configuration

The Galaxy app fetches a package-specific HTTPS configuration hosted by GitHub to manage advertising, new purchase availability and store updates. GitHub receives ordinary connection information such as IP address. Flashlight functionality can work offline using bundled or cached configuration. No downloaded executable code is run. This policy site is also hosted by GitHub; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Local storage

Language, theme, shake preference, Quick Settings tile state, saved light patterns and configuration are stored locally. Android may back up ordinary preferences depending on device settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local data, but does not erase store purchase records or independently retained service data. Restore purchases can recover verified Pro access.

Support and feedback

Contact actions open an editable email draft containing your message, app version, Android version and phone model. Nothing is sent automatically. Sending shares the message and your email address with your email provider and chennoufo11@gmail.com. Use this address for privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support emails.

Terms of Use

FlashDeck controls the flashlight and screen light, including flashing patterns. Availability and brightness depend on the device, camera permission and Android restrictions.

Safety

Flashing lights can affect people with photosensitivity. Avoid flashing near affected people, drivers or moving machinery. Stop if uncomfortable. FlashDeck is not a certified medical or emergency signaling device. Do not rely on it as your only way to request help. Prolonged flashlight use can heat the phone and drain its battery; Android may limit the flash.

Free use and optional Pro

Core flashlight controls remain free. The free Galaxy edition may display ads in Settings. Pro is a one-time, non-subscription Galaxy Store purchase that removes ads and unlocks up to 20 saved patterns and the Neon / Rose theme. The product ID is flashdeck_pro. The price displayed by Galaxy Store applies; confirm any payment in Samsung's payment screen.

Restore purchases using the Samsung account that purchased Pro. Internet is required to purchase and verify ownership. Previously verified Pro access may work offline; a confirmed refund or loss of ownership removes access. Sandbox purchases do not unlock production Pro. Payment and refund requests are handled through Galaxy Store under its applicable terms.

Availability and updates

Advertising and new purchases may be disabled if unavailable or malfunctioning. Restoring previously purchased Pro remains supported. A supported store version may be required for continued compatibility. Updates open the Galaxy Store listing; FlashDeck does not download or install executable updates outside the store.
